# Brand

## Voice

HardEars is locally rooted, slightly self-aware, and unfussy. The Bajan cultural reference does the heavy lifting; the copy around it should be short, dry, and confident.

The brand should not over-explain the joke. People who get it will smile. People who don't will still understand "Bajan speech recognition."

## Tagline directions

Working options:

1. **HardEars — Bajan speech recognition that actually listens.**
2. **HardEars — Bajan ASR.**
3. **HardEars — actually listening.**

The longer tagline (option 1) carries the cultural irony. Option 2 is the safe label. Option 3 is a stripped-back tagline for hero placements.

## What to avoid

- Corporate AI tone ("revolutionising", "empowering", "next-generation").
- Over-explaining "hard ears." Trust the reader.
- Padding the Bajan angle with stereotypes. The phrase is well-attested and the project is serious.
- Generic Caribbean colour palettes or stock imagery unless explicitly chosen.
