## Inspiration

Today's leading speech recognition systems perform well on standard English, but often struggle with Caribbean accents, local vocabulary, place names, code-switching, and fast conversational speech.

In Barbados, valuable information lives in radio, interviews, community media, and everyday spoken conversation. If AI systems cannot accurately understand that speech, much of it remains effectively invisible to search and other AI applications.

HardEars is inspired by the idea that Barbadians shouldn't have to change how they speak to be understood by AI.

## What it does

HardEars is a speech recognition model fine-tuned specifically for Bajan English and Barbadian audio.

Starting from NVIDIA's Nemotron speech model, the aim is to improve transcription accuracy for Bajan accents, local vocabulary, names, places, and expressions.

The wider goal is to make Bajan speech machine-readable, enabling better search, media monitoring, accessibility, cultural preservation, and locally grounded AI applications.

## What's next for HardEars ASR

We plan to build a high-quality Barbados-focused training dataset using real local audio paired with carefully corrected transcripts.

We'll fine-tune NVIDIA Nemotron on this data and evaluate HardEars against the base model, measuring improvements in overall transcription accuracy as well as recognition of local entities, vocabulary, and dialect.

The longer-term goal is to release a model that can provide a strong speech-recognition foundation for Barbados and potentially demonstrate how similar techniques could be applied to other underrepresented Caribbean varieties of English.
